AniKitty 0.7.10 - Streaming, watch UI, and watch parties

STREAMING & PROVIDERS
- Active sources only: Anitaku (Beta), Anikoto (Kilo), MegaPlay (Alpha), AllAnime (Delta), MKissa (Echo), AniNeko (Gamma), AniBD (Hotel).
- All providers use KittyAPI stream-direct with audio=sub or audio=dub (no per-provider server carousel for active sources).
- Fixed wrong anime playing (removed risky auto/stream fallbacks for named providers).
- Dub works when the provider supports it — no longer blocked by incomplete AniList dub counts.
- AniBD stays sub-only; dub missing on an episode retries sub on the same source.
- MegaPlay and Anikoto updated for AniList-first site scrape API (shared anilist-site modules).
- Auto-switch to the next source when playback fails (optional in player settings).

WATCH PAGE UI
- Shared provider accent colors (pills, toolbar chip, info bar, loading skeleton).
- Compact source picker: segmented SUB/DUB toggle and provider dropdown.
- Full provider grid: colored pills and gradient section cards.
- Provider-tinted loading overlay and stream info chips.
- FAQ updated for stream-direct sources, sub/dub behavior, and player settings.

HOMEPAGE
- Fixed Continue Watching overlapping the welcome bar.
- Quick nav and explore bar use unified site-panel / site-chip styling.
- Continue Watching wrapped in site-panel card.
- Discover anchor points at the season browser; feature promos moved to #features.
- Live watch parties rail on home (join open rooms in one tap).

WATCH TOGETHER
- Public room list on /watch-together — browse and join without a code.
- Rooms have names (auto from anime + episode when created from the player).
- Hosts delete rooms for everyone; guests leave without closing the room.
- GET /api/w2g/rooms lists open parties (last 12 hours).
- DB migration: w2g_rooms.name column (0033_w2g_room_name.sql).

OTHER
