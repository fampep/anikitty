🎉 AniKitty v0.6.0 is live!

Big update — reviews, achievements, MAL linking and a lot more.

⭐ RATE & REVIEW ANIME
• Give any anime a 1–10 score and write a review
• Every title now has its own community "AniKitty Score"
• New Reviews tab with helpful votes and spoiler tags
• Earn Kittycoins for your first review of a title

🏆 ACHIEVEMENTS & STREAKS
• Unlock badges for watching, reviewing, commenting and leveling up
• Daily login streaks — bonus Kittycoins every 7 days in a row
• All shown off on your profile

🔗 MYANIMELIST LINKING
• Connect your real MAL account in Settings → Connections

📊 MORE RATINGS & INFO
• MyAnimeList, TMDB, Kitsu and IMDb scores on every anime page
• Rewatch counter on completed titles + your score on your list cards

🎙️ VOICE ACTOR PAGES
• Search actors, browse their full filmography
• Tap any actor on an anime to see all their roles

⬆️ NEW LEVEL TIERS (up to 200!)
• Fresh ranks all the way to level 200
• Prestige tiers above 100: Legend, Sage, Ascendant, Immortal, Deity

✨ IMPROVEMENTS
• Shared links now unfurl with a branded poster + score card on Discord
• "Recently viewed" row + more content on the home page
• Cleaner synopses & VA bios with spoilers hidden until you tap
• Clickable genres, clearer "resume episode" label, smoother tab switches
• Faster home page, reworked status page, new Singapore server node
• Branded 404 page + scroll-to-top button

🐛 FIXES
• Avatars no longer look stretched or squished
• Autoplay now correctly counts down and moves to the next episode
• Skip / next / countdown buttons now show in fullscreen

Thanks for using AniKitty 🐱 — feedback always welcome!
