## ⚠️ WARNING

- This build installs over AniKitty 1.2.8 and the 1.2.9-dev builds. It is signed with the same key as those releases. Your library, watch progress, and AniList login stay on the device.
- Nothing is reset. There is no settings migration and no new permission.
- If Android says the package conflicts, the installed copy was signed with a different key (a debug or unofficial build). Uninstalling that copy deletes its local library. The AniList account itself is unchanged.

## 💥 Breaking changes

None.

## ✨ What's new

### Player
- The page player shows play and skip controls when you tap it, and a pill while Sub or Dub is switching.
- Sub or Dub now plays that audio, including on Chromecast.
- End-of-episode HUD, hold-to-boost, and the level readout are clearer. The sleep timer pauses playback.

### Home
- Continue stays on the episode you were watching, and an episode that has not aired yet does not open.
- The hero waits after you swipe it. Continue cards show a play icon.
- Shelf colors and the catalog cache stay in sync.

### Search
- Filters and empty states follow light and dark, and stay easy to tap.

### My Space
- Light mode is readable. Shelves keep the titles you asked for.
- A watched toggle sends AniList the episode that was stored, retries a failed save, and does not rewind progress you already passed.

### Schedule
- Light and dark both read clearly. Loading uses skeletons, an empty day can jump to the next busy day, and the day list fades when you change it.

### Store and extensions
- kaa.lt, animex.one, and xanime.app are bundled on-device sources. The store format is still protocol 1.1.

### Settings
- Settings and My Space follow the light theme.

### UI polish
- The bottom dock, posters, menus, and profile card follow the theme. Shared controls are 48dp, with spoken names on the main actions.

### Fixes
- Watch progress stays on the episode that was actually playing.
- Lists, the schedule, and AniList progress stay aligned with what you watched.
