**AniKitty 2.0.2** — Scene previews on the seek bar (Beta)

Hover the progress bar and you now see the actual scene you're about to land on — on every provider, not just the handful that ship their own thumbnails.

**Seek previews (Beta)**
- Hover or drag the seek bar to see a preview of that moment, with the timestamp
- Previously this only worked on the few providers that supply their own thumbnails — everywhere else you just got a time bubble
- Now the player quietly grabs the frames itself, so it works across the whole provider list
- Intro / Recap / Credits still get labelled on the preview, and the buffered-ahead readout is unchanged

**Still in beta — what to expect**
- The first hover over a fresh part of the episode can take a moment while it fetches that scene; the nearest frame you've already passed shows in the meantime
- Frames come from the lowest quality rung on purpose — they're thumbnail-sized, and it keeps the cost down
- Desktop only for now. It's off on phones and tablets, and off while casting, so it never competes with playback for bandwidth
- Providers that ship real thumbnails still use those — they're sharper and free

Found a spot where it stalls or shows the wrong scene? Tell us in the Discord — provider + episode helps a lot.
