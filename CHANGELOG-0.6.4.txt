🎉 AniKitty v0.6.4 is live!

GIFs, image uploads, watch history and smoother streaming.

🔍 GIF SEARCH IN COMMENTS & CHAT
• Search Giphy right from the composer — no more pasting links
• Pop-out picker with trending GIFs and instant search
• Tap a GIF to drop it in

🖼️ UPLOAD YOUR AVATAR & BANNER
• Pick an image straight from your device
• Drag to reposition and zoom to frame it exactly how you want
• Saved in high quality (paste-a-link still available as a fallback)

🕘 WATCH HISTORY
• A full, paginated history of everything you've watched
• One-tap resume where you left off, or rewatch finished episodes
• Open it from the account menu

✅ MARK A WHOLE SERIES AS WATCHED
• One button on the anime page moves it to Completed
• Syncs the completed status to AniList too

💬 SUBTITLE TIMING
• New delay control in Settings to nudge subs earlier or later
• Sync subtitles to the audio when they're off

✨ IMPROVEMENTS
• Auto-failover — if a stream fails to play, the player switches to another server automatically instead of getting stuck
• Reduced the dark fade behind the player controls so the video and subtitles stay easy to read
• Added a DMCA / copyright page for takedown requests

🐛 FIXES
• Profile picture frames are now round instead of a square box

Thanks for using AniKitty 🐱 — feedback always welcome!
