AniKitty 0.9.0 - Custom player, Cast/AirPlay, and new providers
Date: 2026-07-19

ADDED
- AniKitty custom player is now the sole watch player (Vidstack removed)
- Google Cast on the control bar — soft subs, auto-next episode, episode poster while casting
- AirPlay for Safari / iOS
- KuroHost and Zenkai (Zulu) as selectable providers; MKissa (Echo) re-enabled
- Drag-to-reorder provider priority in Settings
- Seek preview thumbnails from stream storyboards
- Official AniKitty domains listed on the landing footer

IMPROVED
- Player chrome — captions, quality picker, skip markers, scrub/storyboard, shortcuts help
- Watch page layout — denser player-first composition and clearer mobile playlist
- Cast remint covers every provider path and keeps upstream cookies when needed
- Switzerland and Netherlands CDN nodes back in the race pool

FIXED
- Chromecast hanging or missing soft subtitles on load
- Captions sticking to the top of the frame
- Player scrub / storyboard jank and mobile chrome edges

REMOVED
- Classic Vidstack player toggle
- ReAnime (slot replaced by KuroHost)
- Adcash banner ad placements
